# The site for Escuadra 2.0 — design

Supersedes `2026-09-12-gh-pages-marketing-site-design.md` for the home page and the
shared look. The design canvas, owner-approved on 2026-11-02:
https://claude.ai/artifact/95oDjvvMFE8JFmGrPWBeQC (private to the owner).

## Purpose

2.0 is when Escuadra starts being promoted: Instagram and LinkedIn send fans here,
mostly on phones, and the app's Share text links here too. The page has one job:
make a football fan curious enough to install. It does that by letting them play.

## The home page, top to bottom

1. **The opening.** Home's number wall drifting behind the headline (the app's
   `numberWall`), a lit 7 with the mark striking into its corner (the launch intro's
   shot), the App Store promotional text, both badges.
2. **Try one.** Three level-1 questions played as the app plays them: number,
   clues, four names, instant feedback, then the misses (`quiz.mjs`, pure and tested;
   `site.mjs` draws it). The stage has no photo: the team's bands under a wash, as
   the app shows a question with none. The facts are the app's squads; re-check them
   when squads are refreshed. Ages are worked out from birth dates on the day.
3. **The ladder**, in the app's own level names and unlock rules.
4. **The misses are the point**: Results' cards swiping by themselves, and Study.
5. **Every squad**: 96, 157, 5, and two drifting rows of clubs (colour bands) and
   nations (flag images).
6. **A la escuadra**: the share picture, as the app draws it.
7. **Promises**, the last call, and the footer with the listing's affiliation line
   and the badge credits.

## Decisions

- **No player photos, no crests.** The owner's photo decision covers the store
  listings only. Clubs are bands, never an emblem (guardrail 2); nations use the
  app's flag images (`assets/flags/README.md`).
- **The privacy line says what 2.0 does**: anonymous usage statistics unless
  switched off, with a link to the policy. "No personal data" was a 1.x claim.
- **The Privacy link is `privacy/2/`.** `docs/release.md` moves it once every live
  store ships 2.0, so this page goes live only then.
- **Fonts are the app's own and served from here** (`assets/fonts/README.md`, OFL):
  the site loads nothing from another host. Body text is the visitor's system font,
  which saves about 1 MB of Inter.
- **The privacy pages are not regenerated.** `privacy/2/` comes from main's
  `scripts/privacy-pages.ts`; `site.css` restyles their existing class names
  (`site-header`, `brand-mark`, `theme-toggle`, `legal-page`, `language-nav`,
  `site-footer`), so a later regeneration keeps the look. Rename none of them.
- **English only.** Five languages would put every page through native review.
- **Motion** follows the app: the wall drifts, the cards deal, the mark strikes once,
  and Reduce Motion stills all of it.
