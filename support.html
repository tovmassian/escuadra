Questions

Before you write

How do I unlock levels 2 and 3?

Score 8 out of 10 on level 1 to unlock level 2, and 8 out of 10 on level 2 to unlock level 3. Each team keeps its own ladder.

Why do some players have no photo?

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, and only where Commons has one under a licence Escuadra can use. Without one, or offline before a photo has loaded, the question shows the team’s colours and the shirt number instead.

Does Escuadra work offline?

Yes. Every squad is stored on your phone, so the quiz works without a connection. Photos load from Wikimedia Commons when you’re online.

A shirt number or a player looks wrong.

Squads are compiled from Wikipedia and Wikidata, and each team shows when it was last updated. If something is out of date, open an issue naming the team and the player.

How do I change the language?

Escuadra follows your phone’s language. To pick another, open Settings and then Language: English, Español, Français, Italiano or Deutsch.

How do I switch off usage statistics?

Open Settings and switch off Usage statistics. About, in the app, lists everything that leaves your device, and so does the privacy policy.

How do I start over?

On a team’s screen, the eraser button opens Start over, which sends that team back to unplayed. In Settings, Clear all scores does it for every team, and Reset Escuadra puts everything back to how it was on first launch.
