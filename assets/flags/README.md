# Flag images

A few of the app's national flags (main's `assets/flags/`), shown on the home page
beside nations that have a squad in Escuadra, and on the teaser's nationality clue.
They are copied unchanged, one PNG per FIFA three-letter code.

National flags are the one carve-out from guardrail 2: a flag carries no trademark.
Clubs never get an image; the site shows a club as its colours in bands.
