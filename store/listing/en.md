## Description (both, 4000)

Escuadra is a study tool for football squads.

Pick a team, answer ten questions, and learn who wears which number — with instant feedback and a results screen that shows exactly what you missed. Built for short, repeated sessions: before a tournament, before a season, on the way to the match.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Every club in Europe's top five leagues
• More than 40 national team squads
• Thousands of players, all stored on your device
• Squads are refreshed each season — new teams arrive without an app update

HOW IT WORKS
Level 1 — a shirt number and a few stats; pick the player from four names.
Level 2 — the same, then their position.
Level 3 — the shirt number alone; six names drawn from the same squad, then position, then their club or nationality.

A question is one scored unit. On levels 2 and 3 you only take the point if every part is right — there is no partial credit. The misses are the whole point of the exercise, so the results screen lists the players you got wrong, ready for the next round.

A flawless round is a la escuadra — the shot into the top corner of the goal.

ALSO
• Study mode — browse any full squad: number, name, position, club
• Best score kept per team and per level
• Light and dark themes, following your device setting by default
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection required

ABOUT THE DATA
Squad data is compiled from Wikipedia and reflects the season shown on each team. Wikipedia content is available under CC BY-SA. Escuadra is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any club, national association, league, or competition.
