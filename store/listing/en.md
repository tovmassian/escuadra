## Description (both, 4000)

Escuadra is a study tool for football squads.

Pick a team, answer ten questions, and learn who wears which number — with instant feedback and a results screen that shows exactly what you missed. Built for short, repeated sessions: before a tournament, before a season, on the way to the match.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Every club in Europe's top five leagues
• 157 national team squads
• Thousands of players, all stored on your device
• Player photos from Wikimedia Commons where one is available, each credited
• Squads are refreshed each season — new teams arrive without an app update
• In English, Spanish, French, Italian and German

HOW IT WORKS
Level 1 — a shirt number and a few clues (position, age, and club or nationality); pick the player from four names.
Level 2 — a shirt number, age, and club or nationality; pick the name, then the position.
Level 3 — the shirt number alone; pick the name from six drawn from the same squad, then the position, then the club or nationality.

A question is one scored unit. On levels 2 and 3 you only take the point if every part is right — there is no partial credit. The misses are the whole point of the exercise, so the results screen shows each player you got wrong, to go over before the next round.

A flawless round is called "a la escuadra": Spanish for a shot into the top corner of the goal.

ALSO

• Your squads — star a team and it waits for you on Home

• Light and dark themes, following your device setting by default

PRIVACY
Your scores and settings are stored on your device. The quiz works without a connection: player photos load from Wikimedia Commons when you are online, and the team's colours and the shirt number take the place of any photo not already loaded. Unless you switch off "Usage statistics" in Settings, Escuadra sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service. They include the teams and levels you play, your scores and some settings, and never player names, your answers or anything you type. About, in the app, lists everything that leaves your device.

ABOUT THE DATA
Squad data is compiled from Wikipedia and Wikidata and reflects the season shown on each team. Wikipedia content is available under CC BY-SA, and Wikidata under CC0. Player photos come from Wikimedia Commons, each credited in the app with its author and licence. Escuadra is an independent, unofficial study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any confederation, league or competition, any national football association, any club, or the Wikimedia Foundation, and no player shown in a photo endorses it. Club, national team and confederation names are used for identification only and remain the property of their owners, as do any club marks visible in player photos.
