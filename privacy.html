Escuadra does not collect any personal data about you.

There is no account, no sign-in, and no advertising, analytics or tracking software. Escuadra never sends your scores or settings off your device.

App updates

When Escuadra starts, it checks whether a newer version of the app is available from Expo, the service that delivers its updates. The check sends what is needed to deliver the right update: your device's operating system, the app's version, and a random identifier created for this installation. Escuadra does not link the identifier to you or to anything you do in the app. If the app crashed or failed to load an update while starting up, the next check also sends Expo the technical error message from that failure. Like any online service, Expo also receives your device's IP address with each check. Without a connection, the app works fully offline and skips the check.

What is stored on your device

Escuadra saves three things locally, on your device: your best score for each team and difficulty level, from which it works out which levels you have completed; the last team and level you played; and your light or dark appearance preference. The update check also keeps on your device the random identifier it sends to Expo.

All of this lives in the app's own private storage. Apart from that identifier, Escuadra never sends any of it off your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it from your device. Escuadra has no backup or sync of its own, but your device's backups (on iOS, to iCloud or a computer; on Android, to Google) may include it. The developer has no access to this storage.

Crash reports

If you allow your device to share diagnostics, Apple (on iOS) or Google (on Android) may share anonymised crash reports with the developer. That is handled by Apple or Google and controlled in your device settings. Apart from the error message the update check may send after a failed start, Escuadra itself sends no crash reports.

Children

Escuadra collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
