    Ten out of ten

    A flawless round has a name.

    A la escuadra

    Spanish for a shot into the top corner of the goal. Go ten for ten and Escuadra says so.

    Then share the round as a picture or as text. No player photos, no names — friends can still play it.

    Made for short sessions

    Nothing between you
    and the squad.

    No account. No ads.

    Nothing to sign up for and nothing in the way. Open it and play.

    Plays offline

    Every squad lives on your phone. Photos load from Wikimedia Commons when you’re online; the team’s colours stand in when you’re not.

    Credited photos

    Player photos from Wikimedia Commons where one is available, each with its author and licence.

    Five languages

    English, Español, Français, Italiano and Deutsch. It follows your phone’s language, or you can pick one in Settings.

    Light and dark

    It follows your phone’s appearance, or you can choose one yourself.

    Anonymous statistics you can switch off

    Escuadra sends anonymous usage statistics unless you switch them off in Settings. The privacy policy lists everything that leaves your device.
